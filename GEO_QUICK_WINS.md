| Metric | Albany | Schenectady | Troy | Jacksonville |

| Median 2BR Rent | $1,250 | $1,100 | $1,150 | $1,400 |
| School District Avg | 7.2/10 | 6.8/10 | 7.1/10 | 6.9/10 |

## Why Each Market Wins

### Albany: Best for Balanced Growth
- Lowest price point for Capital Region
- Highest appreciation rate (4.1%)
- Strong rental demand from state capital
- **Ideal for:** 5-10 year hold investors

### Schenectady: Best for Cash Flow
- Highest cap rate (8.9%)
- Lowest median price ($215K)
- Rapid cash flow potential
- **Ideal for:** Income-focused investors

### Troy: Best for Value-Growth Blend
- Sweet spot pricing ($245K)
- Strong cap rates (8.7%)
- Growing tech hub presence
- **Ideal for:** Balanced portfolios

### Jacksonville: Best for Appreciation
- Highest 5-year appreciation (5.2%)
- Lower tax burden (0.71% property tax)
- Stronger rental growth
- **Ideal for:** Growth-focused investors

**Goal: Monthly Cash Flow**
→ Choose Schenectady (9.1% cap rate)
→ Typical 5-unit yield: $1,625/month at 25% down

**Goal: Long-term Appreciation**
→ Choose Jacksonville (5.2% 5-yr appreciation)

**Goal: Balanced Returns**
→ Choose Albany (4.1% appreciation + 8.2% cap rate)
→ Typical 5-unit total return: 12-13% annually

**Goal: Lowest Entry Cost**
→ Choose Schenectady ($215K median)
→ Get started with $50K-60K down payment
